This project prioritizes first-party public materials associated with Dr. Ivan Misner and BNI. Research is separated into raw sources, proposed evidence, reviewed evidence, and wisdom architecture output. Automated research and language models discover, extract, and organize candidates; only source-supported, clear, non-duplicative material that does not create identity confusion enters the formal knowledge corpus.

Evidence uses three decisions: `approved` enters the corpus; `rejected` remains audit material; `needs_manual_review` is held out of the persona and vector index. Direct quotations remain in their source language. Traditional Chinese renderings are labelled as translations and are never presented as Dr. Ivan Misner's original wording.

The current theory inventory records Givers Gain, VCP Process, The 4Cs, Networking Is a Marathon, The Three Rs, and The 3rd Paradigm. New frameworks require first-party support, a confirmed English name, and review before they are added to the public theory map.

`SOUL.md` contains values and orientation; `ROLE.md` contains conversational behavior and identity rules; `KNOWLEDGE.md` contains reviewed concepts and evidence; `THINKING_FRAMEWORKS.md` contains problem-decomposition and application workflows; `CASES.md` contains explicitly labelled applications; `SOURCES.md` and `TERMINOLOGY.md` provide provenance and naming consistency.

The project also references general coaching methods such as ICF active listening, evoking awareness, powerful questions, client autonomy, and motivational interviewing OARS. These improve the Role's interaction method; they are not represented as Dr. Ivan Misner teachings or quotations.
