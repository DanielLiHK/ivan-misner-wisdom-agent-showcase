# Research Method / 研究方法

## 繁體中文

本 project 以 Ivan Misner 及 BNI 的公開第一方資料為主要來源，將研究結果分成 raw source、proposed evidence、reviewed evidence 及 persona output。自動研究和語言模型只負責發現、抽取和整理候選內容；只有可由來源核對、概念清晰、沒有重複及沒有身份誤導的資料，才可進入正式 knowledge corpus。

Evidence 採用三分法：`approved` 進入正式 corpus；`rejected` 只保留作內部 audit；`needs_manual_review` 暫停，不進 persona 或 vector index。正式 quote 必須保留原文，任何繁中版本都標示為中文翻譯，不會冒充 Ivan Misner 原句。

目前 theory inventory 已記錄 Givers Gain®、VCP Process、The 4Cs、Networking Is a Marathon、The Three Rs 及 The 3rd Paradigm。其他框架只會在找到第一方來源、確認英文名稱及核對實質內容後才新增 concept ID。使用者提到的「房間理論」目前只作候選例子，不代表 project 已確認存在此正式理論。

`SOUL.md` 放價值方向與智慧取向；`ROLE.md` 放對話行為及身份規則；`KNOWLEDGE.md` 放已核實概念及 evidence；`THINKING_FRAMEWORKS.md` 放拆題與應用流程；`CASES.md` 放明確標示的應用建議；`SOURCES.md` 和 `TERMINOLOGY.md` 分別負責可追溯性和名稱一致性。

本 project 另外參考通用 coaching 方法，例如 ICF 的 active listening、evoking awareness、powerful questions、client autonomy，以及 motivational interviewing 的 OARS。這些只用來改善 Role 的互動方法，不會被寫成 Ivan Misner 的理論或原話。

## English

This project prioritizes first-party public materials associated with Ivan Misner and BNI. Research is separated into raw sources, proposed evidence, reviewed evidence, and persona output. Automated research and language models discover, extract, and organize candidates; only source-supported, clear, non-duplicative material that does not create identity confusion enters the formal knowledge corpus.

Evidence uses three decisions: `approved` enters the corpus; `rejected` remains audit material; `needs_manual_review` is held out of the persona and vector index. Direct quotations remain in their source language. Traditional Chinese renderings are labelled as translations and are never presented as Ivan Misner's original wording.

The current theory inventory records Givers Gain®, VCP Process, The 4Cs, Networking Is a Marathon, The Three Rs, and The 3rd Paradigm. New frameworks require first-party support, a confirmed English name, and review before receiving a concept ID. The user's example of a “room theory” is only a candidate placeholder; the project does not assume that a formal theory exists under that name.

`SOUL.md` contains values and orientation; `ROLE.md` contains conversational behavior and identity rules; `KNOWLEDGE.md` contains reviewed concepts and evidence; `THINKING_FRAMEWORKS.md` contains problem-decomposition and application workflows; `CASES.md` contains explicitly labelled applications; `SOURCES.md` and `TERMINOLOGY.md` provide provenance and naming consistency.

The project also references general coaching methods such as ICF active listening, evoking awareness, powerful questions, client autonomy, and motivational interviewing OARS. These improve the Role's interaction method; they are not represented as Ivan Misner teachings or quotations.
