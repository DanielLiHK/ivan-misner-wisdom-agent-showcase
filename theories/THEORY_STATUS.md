## Evidence discipline

`Pass with gaps` means the available public first-party material supports a cautious foundation, not that the theory has been exhaustively researched. `Needs research` means this release deliberately limits the claim surface. The project does not treat model-generated wording or raw-page placeholders as evidence.

## Identity discipline

Theories, trademarks, book titles, and public teachings remain associated with their respective sources and rights holders. This file is a research summary, not an official theory catalogue.
