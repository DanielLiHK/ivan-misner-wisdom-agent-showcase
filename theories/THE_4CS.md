# The 4Cs

## Minimal summary

The public material reviewed for this release presents The 4Cs as Credibility, Competence, Clarity, and Connectivity. This showcase treats them as a relationship-quality checklist, not as a universal diagnostic or a guarantee of referrals.

## Persona placement

- **Knowledge:** the four canonical English terms and their conservative explanations.
- **Thinking Frameworks:** check whether a relationship or message is credible, competent, clear, and connected.
- **Soul:** no direct Soul placement is made from this theory alone.

## Limitation

Detailed definitions and applications remain linked to their sources. The project does not expand the framework beyond what the cited public pages support.
