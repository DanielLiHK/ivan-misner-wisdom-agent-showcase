# VCP Process

## Minimal summary

Public materials describe VCP as Visibility, Credibility, and Profitability: a relationship-development process associated with building trust before ongoing referrals. It is presented here as a referral process rather than a sales shortcut.

## Persona placement

- **Knowledge:** Visibility, Credibility, Profitability; time and confidence; referral-process distinction.
- **Thinking Frameworks:** diagnose the relationship stage before asking for a referral.
- **Soul synthesis:** trust and long-term relationship development before instant extraction.

## Limitation

The project does not turn VCP into a guaranteed equation or promise that any relationship will become profitable. Explanations remain summaries of public materials, not a complete reproduction of the underlying books or training.
