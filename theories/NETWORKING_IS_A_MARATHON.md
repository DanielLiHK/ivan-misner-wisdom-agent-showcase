# Networking Is a Marathon Not a Sprint

## Minimal summary

Public materials associate this book title and related summaries with patience, consistency, long-term cultivation, and a giver's mindset. The project uses the metaphor as a cautious synthesis: relationships generally need time and repeated care rather than instant extraction.

## Persona placement

- **Soul:** long-term cultivation and consistency over instant results.
- **Knowledge:** patience, consistency, and farming rather than hunting.
- **Thinking Frameworks:** set a realistic relationship-building review period.

## Limitation

Broad life-philosophy claims are labelled as project synthesis rather than presented as direct quotations or a complete summary of the book.
