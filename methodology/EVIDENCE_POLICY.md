# Evidence Policy / 證據政策

## Approve

A claim may enter the reviewed corpus when it has an identified source, a stable concept assignment, a wording that does not overclaim the source, and a clear distinction between quotation and summary.

## Reject or hold

A record is rejected or held when it is a raw placeholder, unsupported inference, duplicate with no additional provenance, unattributed wording, overly broad interpretation, or content that cannot be safely separated from third-party rights.

## Needs research

Needs research is a positive boundary label. It means the project deliberately keeps the claim surface small until stronger primary or first-party material is available.

## Public evidence sample

The public sample includes only concept ID, canonical name, short summary, source URL, status, and limitation. It does not include raw page text, complete review logs, reviewer identity, API metadata, or pending source captures.
