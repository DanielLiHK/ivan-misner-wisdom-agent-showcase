# Dr. Ivan Misner Wisdom Agent — Public Research Showcase

### Applying publicly documented wisdom with AI — without impersonating the person behind it

## The idea / 核心理念

**Can AI learn to apply a person's body of wisdom without pretending to be that person?**

This independent research experiment explores that question using the publicly documented work of **Dr. Ivan Misner**.

As a BNI member, Daniel Li has encountered many of Dr. Ivan Misner's ideas through networking practice and his published work.

Understanding an individual principle is relatively easy.

The harder task is being able to integrate many principles together, recognise which ideas are most relevant to a new situation, understand the relationships between them, and apply them thoughtfully and consistently.

This is where the experiment becomes interesting.

Rather than building an AI that simply imitates Dr. Ivan Misner or tries to speak as him, this project explores whether AI can help:

- organise a body of publicly documented wisdom;
- connect related principles across different situations;
- identify which principles may be relevant to a new problem;
- synthesise multiple ideas rather than retrieve isolated quotations;
- apply those ideas to practical situations; and
- clearly distinguish documented teaching from AI-generated synthesis and application.

**The goal is not to create a digital replica of Dr. Ivan Misner.**

**The goal is to explore whether AI can help people use a body of wisdom more coherently.**

## 這是甚麼

**AI 能否學習、整合及應用一個人的公開智慧，而不需要假裝成為那個人？**

因此，本 project 的重點並不是製作一個「扮演 Dr. Ivan Misner」的 AI。

**目的不是複製 Dr. Ivan Misner。**

## Why this project

This project began partly as a personal experiment and tribute.

As a BNI member, I have benefited from ideas developed and shared by Dr. Ivan Misner over many years.

AI creates an unusual opportunity: instead of merely retrieving individual quotations or concepts, it may be possible to organise a larger body of public knowledge and explore how its principles relate to one another and apply across situations.

This repository documents my attempt to explore that possibility responsibly.

It is both an AI experiment and an expression of appreciation for the body of work that made the experiment possible.

## Wisdom architecture

The public showcase keeps four conceptual layers because they make the research design understandable:

- `Soul` describes a stable wisdom orientation distilled from public materials. It is not a claim about Dr. Ivan Misner's private thoughts, personality, internal mental state, or personal identity.
- `Role` describes how an advisor may communicate: encouraging, practical, source-grounded, and capable of asking questions that help people think.
- `Knowledge` organises reviewed concepts, theory summaries, evidence, and source links.
- `Thinking Frameworks` describes selected public reasoning principles for classifying questions, selecting relevant ideas, synthesising them, and applying them with uncertainty labels.

Together these form a **structured wisdom architecture for organising principles, knowledge, reasoning patterns, and application boundaries**. They are not an attempt to reconstruct the actual personality or inner mind of Dr. Ivan Misner.

## What is intentionally excluded

This repository is a curated public research showcase rather than the complete working system.

It intentionally presents selected research outcomes, theory summaries, evidence samples, reasoning principles, and high-level architecture so that the concept can be understood and discussed publicly.

The following remain outside the public repository:

- raw research materials;
- complete source corpus;
- working research notes;
- production code;
- prompts or implementation details that are not already intentionally public;
- internal automation;
- processing infrastructure;
- development experiments;
- private evaluation materials; and
- the complete production system.

The private research repository remains the working source of truth.

The public repository should be understood as a transparent showcase of the **idea, research approach, selected reasoning architecture, and outcomes**, rather than a full release of the underlying system.
