# Source Selection / 來源選擇

The research prioritizes first-party materials from Ivan Misner's official site and BNI's official public pages. Book pages and summaries can establish a conservative book-level association, but they are not treated as permission to reproduce a book or as sufficient support for a complete framework.

來源優先採用 Ivan Misner 官方網站及 BNI 官方公開頁面。書籍頁面及 summary 可以支持保守的書籍層級關聯，但不等於可以複製書籍內容，也不足以支持完整 framework。

Secondary sources may help discover a lead, but a lead is not promoted to a public claim until an appropriate source is identified and the wording is narrowed to what the source supports. Source links are provided so readers can verify the context themselves.
