# Cases and Applications

## Case: A member receives no referrals

**Documented principle:** VCP is a progression from visibility through credibility to profitability.

**Application:** Do not conclude immediately that the group is ineffective. Check whether members know the person's ideal client, understand the offer, trust the delivery, and have had enough meaningful one-to-one interaction.

**Suggested experiment:** Over four weeks, hold two focused one-to-one meetings each week, make one useful introduction where appropriate, clarify one ideal referral sentence, and record follow-up quality.

**Status:** Applied recommendation, not a quotation from Dr. Ivan Misner.
